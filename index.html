01 The Problem

Endurance is the ceiling on payload drones.

Globally

Payload drones are limited by endurance. Battery energy density has plateaued, so the next meaningful gains in flight time can no longer come from the pack. They have to come from the propulsion system that spends it.

That puts the motor, the propeller and the control driving them back at the centre of the problem.

In India

No domestic company manufactures a full-stack UAV propulsion system built for long endurance under payload. Indian builders import it instead, at high cost and from a heavily concentrated source.

≈80%

of imported drone propulsion originates from China, a dependency India needs to end on cost grounds and on strategic ones.

02 Our Solution

One propulsion stack, co-optimised end to end.

Galewings designs the motor, the propeller and the control algorithms as a single system, and validates them together. Matching them to each other, instead of bolting together three parts that were each optimised alone, is where the endurance comes from.

  1. Motor

    Electromagnetic and mechanical design carried out in-house, on high-quality materials.

  2. Propeller

    Matched to the motor’s operating envelope rather than selected from a catalogue.

  3. Control

    Drive algorithms developed in-house and tuned against the motor they run on.

The result is longer endurance at lower cost than imported alternatives, manufactured in India.

03 Technology

Validated on the bench, not on a datasheet.

The motor is bench-validated. In comparative testing it has demonstrated efficiency exceeding that of major established motor manufacturers.

Efficiency comparisons are measured at system level (motor plus propeller), as propeller configurations differ across manufacturers.

We do not publish detailed test data on this page. We walk through it directly, with context, in a technical briefing.

Electromagnetic design
The motor’s magnetic design is developed in-house, not sourced as a module.
Mechanical design
Mechanical design and material selection are done in-house, for the loads the system actually carries.
Control algorithms
The drive algorithms are written in-house and tuned against the motor they run on.
